The complete business compliance checklist for Kenya

Across BRS, KRA, DOSHS, NEMA, SHA, NSSF and the ODPC · general guidance, not legal advice

Running a compliant business in Kenya means answering to several regulators at once, each with its own registrations, returns and renewal dates. This checklist pulls them into one place so nothing falls through the cracks. Use it as a starting map, then track the live dates for your own business.

1. Company & business registration (BRS)

Register the company with the Business Registration Service, keep the registered details current, and file annual returns every year. Companies that don't file risk penalties and being struck off. Also obtain your county single business permit and renew it annually, plus any sector-specific licences.

2. Tax (KRA)

Register for a PIN and the right tax obligations on iTax. Depending on your business that means PAYE (monthly, by the 9th), VAT if registered (monthly, by the 20th), instalment tax (quarterly), turnover tax for smaller businesses, and the annual income tax return. Keep records to support every return.

3. Payroll statutory deductions

For every employee: NSSF (6% + 6%), SHIF (2.75% of gross, replacing NHIF) and the Affordable Housing Levy (1.5% + 1.5%), all remitted monthly. See the dedicated guide: NSSF, SHIF, PAYE & Housing Levy for employers.

4. Workplace safety (DOSHS)

Register the workplace with DOSHS, run an annual OSH audit and annual fire safety audit, maintain WIBA cover, set up a safety committee if you have 20+ staff, and keep first-aid and PPE provision in place. Full detail: OSHA 2007 audit requirements.

5. Environment (NEMA)

Where applicable, hold an EIA licence, run the annual environmental audit, and keep effluent discharge and waste licences current. Full detail: NEMA environmental compliance.

6. Data protection (ODPC)

Under the Data Protection Act, 2019, register with the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner as a data controller or processor where required, and put the required safeguards, consent and breach-handling in place if you hold personal data.
